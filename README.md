# 📚 BookHaven

> "One more chapter..."
>
> _Three hours later:_ "Why is the sun coming up?"

Welcome to **BookHaven**, the friendliest corner of the internet for people who keep buying books despite owning an unread mountain of them.

---

## 🌟 What is BookHaven?

BookHaven is a modern library application built with **Laravel**, **Filament**, and **Livewire**.

It's designed for readers who want to:

- 📖 Discover books
- 🙋 Request books
- 📚 Borrow books
- ⭐ Rate books
- 💬 Leave reviews
- 📊 Keep track of their reading journey

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📚 Browse Books

Find your next favorite book... or your next unfinished one.

- Beautiful responsive catalog
- Detailed book pages
- Ratings and reviews
- Search-friendly interface

---

### 🙋 Request a Book

Saw something interesting?

Click **Request** and politely ask the library for it.

No carrier pigeons required.

---

### 📥 Borrow Books

When your request is approved:

- Borrow the book
- Enjoy reading
- Forget what sleep feels like

---

### 📤 Return Books

Eventually...

Books don't magically teleport back onto shelves.

Return them so someone else can procrastinate with them.

---

### ⭐ Rate Books

Finished reading?

Leave a rating from:

⭐ "Life changing"

to

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ "Stayed awake until 3 AM."

_(Okay... technically they're all stars.)_

---

### 💬 Reviews

Tell everyone what you thought.

Spoilers? Please don't.

---

### 📊 Personal Dashboard

Track your reading journey:

- Books requested
- Books currently borrowed
- Books returned
- Reading statistics
- Proof that buying books and reading books are two separate hobbies

---

### 🌙 Dark Mode

For those "just one more chapter" sessions at 2 AM.

Your eyes will thank you.
